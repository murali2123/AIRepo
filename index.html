The Parent Privacy Promise

Their memories are
not our raw material.

Children’s photos are sensitive. Little Lantern is designed so a verified adult controls collection, use, sharing and deletion.

  • 01
    No AI trainingUploaded photos are never sold or used to train models.
  • 02
    Minimum dataUse a nickname and age range—never a school, address or birth date.
  • 03
    Automatic deletionOriginal photos expire after creation; parents can delete sooner.
  • 04
    Private sharingNo public gallery. Family links expire and can be revoked.

Production safety model

Privacy before pixels.

Collect

Adult account, verified consent, nickname and age band only. Photo remains optional.

Protect

Strip metadata, scan uploads, encrypt with per-object keys, and use short-lived signed access.

Generate

Approved vendors receive only what the job needs, under no-training and deletion terms.

Delete

Automatic retention limits, provider deletion callbacks, parent controls and auditable tombstones.

A production launch needs specialist legal review for COPPA, applicable state privacy laws, UK Children’s Code and other markets served. This prototype is product design, not legal advice.
